Essential Vendor Identifiers
Vendor Code/ID: Unique identifier for the vendor in your system

Legal Vendor Name: Full registered business name as it appears on official documents

GST Registration Number: Critical for tax validation and compliance checks​

PAN Number: For Indian tax compliance and vendor verification

TAN Number: Tax deduction account number

Contact & Address Information
Registered Business Address: Complete address with PIN code

Billing Address: If different from registered address

Phone Number: Primary contact number

Email Address: For invoice correspondence​

Banking & Payment Details
Bank Account Number: For payment processing

IFSC Code: Bank branch identifier

Payment Terms: Standard payment cycles (e.g., Net 30, Net 60)

Preferred Payment Method: NEFT, RTGS, cheque, etc.​

Business Classification
Vendor Category/Type: Services, goods, utilities, etc.

HSN/SAC Codes: For the types of goods/services they typically supply​

Tax Registration Status: Whether registered under GST, composition scheme, etc.

Compliance & Verification
MSME Registration: If applicable for compliance

Registration Certificates: Copy of GST certificate, incorporation certificate

Average Market Pricing: Historical pricing data for anomaly detection​
