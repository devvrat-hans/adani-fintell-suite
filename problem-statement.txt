Problem statement #1: AI Agent for Expense Anomaly & Compliance
Background
At Adani Finance, thousands of expense claims, vendor invoices, pay disbursements, and
procurement spends are processed every month. Today, many of these checks are manual
and reactive, which slows down operations and risks of missing fraud or compliance issues.
With advances in AI, we can now imagine an autonomous finance agent that not only
extracts scanned images and stores data in structured form but also validates the content of
the Invoices.
The Challenge
Build an AI Agent (put a cool name )that solves the following:
Uses AI-powered OCR to extract following invoice data from PDF.JPEG, etc format
uploaded in a folder:
a. Invoice number
b. Invoice amount
c. Invoice date
d. GST registration number of vendor
e. GST registration number of the billed company
HSN/SAC code
f. Description/content of items billed
g. Achieves extraction accuracy above 80%.
h. Identifies and flags duplicate invoices in processed sets.
i. Validates vendor and company GST registration numbers directly from the government
GST portal.
j. Checks if billed GST rates match regulatory rates according to HSN/SAC codes.
k. Checks Arithmetical accuracy of the Invoice
l. Compares billed product prices to the average market price (using AI grounding) and flags
outliers.

Core Capabilities
1. Ingest & Extract Data
a. Accept invoice files (PDF, JPEG, PNG, etc.) from a designated folder.
b. Extract structured data fields as specified, with over 80% extraction accuracy.
c. Parse item-wise details including HSN/SAC code and description from invoice content.

2. Anomaly & Compliance Checks
- Detect duplicate invoices through document field and content matching.
- Validate GST numbers using live checks on government portals.

- Check GST rates using applicable HSN/SAC codes to ensure compliant billing.
- Compare item prices against AI-derived market benchmarks, highlighting anomalies for
manual review.
- Check Arithmetical accuracy of the Invoice and highlight differences.

3. Expected Output (Hackathon Prototype)

a. System automatically detects, and processes uploaded invoices.
b. Extracted invoice information is displayed with at least 80% field accuracy.
c. The interface highlights:
d. Duplicate invoices
e. Any invalid or mismatched GST details
f. Incorrect rates by HSN/SAC code
g. Price discrepancies compared to market rates

5. Flag & Visualize
a) Provide a simple dashboard or chatbot interface showing anomalies, risk scores, etc.
b) Allow natural language queries: “Show me top 2 anomalies observed in duplicate
Invoice”

Why It Matters
a) Reduces manual effort and audit fatigue.
b) Improves fraud detection and compliance monitoring.
c) Learns continuously becoming more accurate and valuable with usage.
d) Positions finance as data-driven, proactive, and AI-augmented.

Tech Guidance (just sample – feel free to use what you
want)
 OCR & Document AI: Tesseract, Google Document AI, AWS Textract, Azure Form
Recognizer.
 Anomaly Detection: Clustering, statistical outliers, autoencoders.
 Learning: Train on historical transaction data, update with feedback.
 Agent Frameworks: LangChain, LlamaIndex, or similar.
 Interfaces: Dashboards (Streamlit, Power BI) or conversational bots (Teams, Slack).


Expected Output (Hackathon Prototype)
 Upload invoices (images/PDFs) into the system / automatically pick them up from
email/share drive.
 AI Agent extracts data, runs compliance + anomaly checks.
 Dashboard or conversational interface highlights risky transactions with explanations.
 Evidence of learning patterns over time (e.g., reduced false positives, vendor spend
profiles).
