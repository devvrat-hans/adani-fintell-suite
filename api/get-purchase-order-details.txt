Field Descriptions:
- po_id: Unique purchase order identifier
- po_number: Purchase order number
- po_date: Date when PO was created
- po_version: Version number for amendments
- po_status: Current status (Draft, Approved, Closed, Cancelled)
- vendor_reference: Complete vendor information
- delivery_details: Delivery address and dates
- line_items: Array of items with pricing and GRN details
- financial_summary: Complete financial breakdown
- payment_terms: Payment cycle and due dates
- approval_workflow: Requester and approver details
- budget_allocation: Cost center and project codes
- contract_reference: Associated contract details
- invoice_matching: Linked invoices and matching status
- metadata: Creation and update information

Notes:
- All dates are in ISO 8601 format
- Amounts are in INR (Indian Rupees)
- GRN details will be null if goods not yet received
- Matching status can be: "Pending", "Partially Matched", "Fully Matched"
